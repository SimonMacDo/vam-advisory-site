# Content and SEO plan

## Intended search coverage

- VAM Advisory
- VAM Advisory Hong Kong
- VAM Advisory Limited (verify the registered name before publication)
- Vera Tan
- Vera Tan VAM Advisory
- Relevant consulting and advisory services, to be confirmed with the business

These are planning objectives, not guarantees of ranking. Use natural, informative copy rather than keyword repetition.

## Content requirements

1. Home: approved positioning, intended clients, location and links to services and advisers.
2. Services: verified offerings and meaningful answers to client needs. Add dedicated service pages when there is enough distinct content.
3. Team: approved introductions with links to individual profiles.
4. Vera Tan profile: verified name, role, expertise, biography, qualifications, photograph and relevant professional links. Link to the profile from Home and Team where appropriate.
5. Contact: confirmed business name, location and contact methods. No functioning form or email integration is included in this scaffold.

## Before production launch

- Audit existing URLs, titles, copy, backlinks and available search-performance data in read-only mode.
- Approve the business positioning and corporate identity.
- Create unique page titles, descriptions, headings and internal links.
- Add accurate Organization and Person structured data after verifying the underlying facts.
- Set production canonical URLs only after the domain and launch are approved.
- Populate sitemap.xml with approved indexable production URLs only.
- Prepare redirects from any changed existing URLs and check links and accessible page structure.
- Replace development robots rules and remove noindex only from approved production pages. Keep all staging environments protected and excluded from search.
- Verify production indexing and Search Console access after a separately approved launch.

No domain, DNS, nameserver, email or hosting changes are authorized by this initial repository setup.
