Our experience.
In practice.

A selection of engagements across financial-services compliance, documentation and operations.

These summaries are drawn from VAM’s 2026 capability presentation. Client identities are withheld.

Case 01 / KYC, AML & outsourcing

KYC remediation at scale

Large Chinese state-owned brokerage

75,000client records / two weeks

The requirement

The brokerage needed to remediate client KYC records within an SFC deadline to support participation in Greater Bay Area Wealth Management Connect.

VAM’s contribution

VAM carried out remediation on 75,000 client records in two weeks. The engagement subsequently developed into ongoing outsourced KYC and client account-opening support.

Case 02 / Licensing, suitability & documentation

A framework for virtual-asset trading

Large Chinese state-owned brokerage

The requirement

The brokerage required suitability, training and documentation support for a virtual-asset licensing uplift.

VAM’s contribution

VAM developed a suitability framework and online training, alongside relevant risk disclosures and disclaimers for virtual-asset trading.

Case 03 / KYC, AML & compliance frameworks

Compliance across sales & trading

Large Chinese state-owned bank

The requirement

The bank required a review of existing client KYC and AML records and policies across its sales and trading business lines.

VAM’s contribution

VAM carried out KYC and AML review and remediation in line with SFC requirements and drafted policies and manuals covering multiple products and asset classes.

Case 04 / Product governance & implementation

From product approval to implementation

Large international private bank

The requirement

A private-banking business unit needed a clear process for approving, reviewing and rolling out new structured products.

VAM’s contribution

VAM developed and implemented the approval process with technology support, worked across departments on integration, and developed the supporting policies and key operating procedures.
