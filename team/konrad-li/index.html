Experience and expertise

Global Markets operations and implementation.

Konrad has over 20 years of experience specialising in financial services. The biography previously published by VAM Advisory describes his role as Director and his earlier position as Chief Operating Officer of Bank of America Merrill Lynch’s equities business line.

He has held senior positions supporting Global Markets operations across a range of Asia-Pacific jurisdictions, covering the acquisition and establishment of business operations as well as project remediation.

His product and process experience includes cash equities, derivatives, futures, options, fixed income, currencies and commodities (FICC), and prime brokerage. His expertise also covers client management, analytics, regulatory frameworks and corporate governance.

Konrad holds an honours MBA in Finance from SUNY Buffalo and an undergraduate degree in Computer Information Systems.
