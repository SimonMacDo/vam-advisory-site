Background

A career at the intersection of law and finance.

Vera Tan founded VAM Advisory to give clients access to the kind of senior, cross-disciplinary judgment that complex financial-services decisions require. Her experience connects law, financial markets, compliance, investment and practical implementation.

Vera has more than 25 years of experience in law and banking. She graduated with an honours law degree from University College London, where she received the Bentham Prize for High Achievement. She was called to the UK Bar and Hong Kong Bar in 2001 and practised as a barrister in Hong Kong until 2005.

She subsequently held senior positions across financial markets, compliance, investment and asset management. That breadth informs VAM’s work on in-house legal documentation, financial-services compliance advisory, product suitability, AML/KYC client onboarding, transaction monitoring and remediation support.
