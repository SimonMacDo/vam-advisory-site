01 / Our expertise

In-house legal documentation & litigation support

Practical support for commercial documentation and the coordination of Hong Kong litigation.

Commercial documentation

Support with commercial agreements, ISDA agreements, master confirmations, distribution agreements, fund documents, placing agreements, side letters, risk disclosures and disclaimers.

Litigation coordination

Support with understanding and organising litigation matters, coordinating with solicitors and counsel, and managing the documentation and process around a dispute.

The scope of each engagement is agreed in advance. Litigation representation is handled by separately engaged solicitors and counsel.

02 / Our expertise

Regulatory licensing & compliance

Guidance and hands-on support for financial-services licensing and compliance requirements.

Licensing applications

Support for SFC licensing applications, including virtual-asset-related licensing work, and HKEX / HKFE trading rights.

Compliance frameworks

Compliance manuals, policies, key operating procedures, suitability assessments, independent compliance testing and regulatory inspection support.

03 / Our expertise

KYC, AML & client onboarding

Review, remediation and ongoing support across the client lifecycle.

Review and remediation

KYC and AML reviews, client account-opening checks, screening and remediation of existing client records.

Operational delivery

Workflow, policies and procedures for onboarding, transaction screening, monitoring and suspicious-transaction review and reporting.

04 / Our expertise

Operations & outsourcing

Experienced support for financial-services operations and business projects.

Ongoing support

Trained teams for account-opening verification and KYC / AML processes for brokerages and banks.

Project delivery

Consultants with front-, middle- and back-office experience for project work, implementation and longer-term operational support.

05 / Our expertise

Remediation & corporate governance

Address findings and turn recommendations into practical changes.

Closing findings
