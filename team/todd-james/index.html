Experience and expertise

International markets and private banking.

Todd has over 20 years of international banking experience spanning risk management, debt capital markets, derivatives trading and investments. He began his career as a derivatives analyst at Royal Bank of Canada in Toronto, before moving to the United Kingdom to focus on debt capital markets and derivatives. His previous roles included Director, European Derivatives Marketing at Royal Bank of Scotland.

In 2001, Todd moved to Asia and joined HSBC Global Markets, developing structured investments for retail and high-net-worth investors throughout the region. In 2006, he moved to HSBC Private Bank as Head of Investment Advisory Group in Hong Kong. His subsequent experience includes leading investment services, advisory and discretionary functions at boutique Swiss private banks.

He is the author of Interest Rate Derivatives: A Practical Approach, published by Risk Books in 2006. Todd has provided expert witness testimony in civil matters involving high-net-worth individuals, and regularly conducts training and seminars covering global markets, compliance and wealth management.
